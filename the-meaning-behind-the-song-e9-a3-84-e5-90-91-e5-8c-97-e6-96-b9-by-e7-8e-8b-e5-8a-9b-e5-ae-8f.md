## Symbolism and Metaphors

### Winter Light and Frost  

Winter imagery appears throughout: “the frost on the window,” “the pale glow of streetlamps.” Winter is traditionally linked to *stillness and clarity*, but also to *painful exposure*. By juxtaposing a cold environment with the act of moving north, the song suggests that growth often requires shedding comfort, confronting the harshness of reality, and emerging with a renewed sense of purpose. The gentle piano that glints like ice on a lake captures this duality—beauty and brittleness interwoven.

### The Broken Compass  

In the bridge, the narrator mentions a compass that “spins without direction.” This broken instrument speaks to the *inner disorientation* many feel during life’s turning points. Rather than seeing the malfunction as a failure, the lyric reframes it as an invitation to *trust instinct over external validation*. The metaphor encourages listeners to find their own magnetic north, even when traditional tools feel unreliable.
