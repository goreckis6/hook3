The first time *Beings* drifted through a set and caught the light of a late‑night crowd, it felt less like a typical EDM anthem and more like a quiet confession whispered from behind a wall of synths. Madeon layers bright arpeggios over a pulse that feels simultaneously buoyant and restrained, prompting listeners to wonder what is being hidden beneath the glossy surface. The track asks a simple, yet unsettling question: **who do we become when we are forced to merge our private selves with an ever‑watching collective?** It is a song about the tension between individuality and the pressure to conform, an inner dialogue that resonates with anyone who has ever felt their identity being reshaped by external expectations.

## Key Takeaways
- **Identity under pressure:** *Beings* portrays the struggle of preserving selfhood while being molded by a larger group.
- **Emotional duality:** The music balances hope and melancholy, reflecting both the allure and the loss that come with assimilation.
- **Metaphoric “beings”:** The title works as a metaphor for multiple versions of the self that coexist in a single person.
- **Production as narrative:** Shifting textures and dynamic builds mirror the pull between personal desire and communal demand.
- **Listener resonance:** Fans interpret the track as a soundtrack for moments of self‑reflection in an age of social media scrutiny.
- **Hopeful resolution:** Despite the tension, the track hints at a possible synthesis where individuality can survive within the collective.

### The emotional core of the song

At its heart, *Beings* is a conversation between **the narrator and their own fragmented self**. The opening synths feel tentative, almost shy, suggesting a voice that is hesitant to be heard. As the beat gains momentum, a sense of urgency emerges, as if the narrator is racing to claim a place in a space that feels both welcoming and hostile. The emotional arc moves from **vulnerability**—the fear of being seen as just another “being”—to a **quiet determination** to assert a unique presence. This oscillation captures the feeling of standing on a stage where the lights are bright, yet the crowd blends every individual into a single, indistinguishable mass.

The fear expressed is not a simple dread of rejection; it is the deeper anxiety that one’s *essence* might dissolve when forced to fit the template of a collective. Listeners report moments where the chord progression seems to echo the breathlessness of trying to keep personal dreams alive while the world pushes a more uniform rhythm. The emotional drive that fuels the track is therefore a mixed cocktail of **loneliness**, **yearning for connection**, and **defiant hope** that the self can endure.

### Main themes and message

*Beings* weaves several interlocking themes:

1. **Collective identity vs. personal autonomy** – The recurring motif of “beings” hints at a crowd that is simultaneously composed of individuals and a single organism. This duality raises questions about how much of ourselves we surrender when we become part of a group, whether it be a social circle, a fandom, or a digital community.

2. **Self‑realization amidst conformity** – Throughout the song, there’s an undercurrent pushing the listener toward introspection. The lyrical subtext suggests that recognizing our own “beings” is the first step toward breaking free from imposed molds.

3. **Digital alienation** – Although not overtly political, the electronic texture and repetitive motifs evoke the way algorithms and online platforms can reduce us to data points—“beings” defined by numbers rather than nuance.

4. **Transformation through acceptance** – The track does not end in outright rebellion; instead, the final melodic release feels like an acceptance that individuality and community can coexist, albeit in a transformed state.

These themes converge on a central proposition: **our identity is not static but a fluid assemblage of selves that we must constantly negotiate**. The song becomes a meditation on the ongoing process of redefining who we are while navigating external expectations.

### Symbolism and metaphors

The title *Beings* itself is the most overt metaphor, but its layers run deeper:

- **“Beings” as masks** – Each “being” can be seen as a mask we don a for various social contexts. The lyric imagery of shifting shapes and luminous forms reinforces the idea that we are constantly swapping masks to fit in.

- **Light and shadows** – The bright synth leads represent the shining, visible facets of a person—the parts they want the world to see. In contrast, the darker bass undercurrents symbolize hidden insecurities or suppressed aspects that linger beneath the surface.

- **The pulsating beat** – The relentless kick drum mirrors the steady pressure of societal rhythm. It is a metaphor for the *heartbeat of the crowd* that can either energize or suffocate an individual's tempo.

- **Arpeggiated climbs** – Those upward‑moving melodic lines evoke an ascent, suggesting aspiration to rise above generic categorization and reach a personal summit.

When listeners envision these symbols, the song becomes an auditory painting where each electronic brushstroke hints at an inner landscape riddled with both **visibility and concealment**.

### The role of the title and hook

The repetitive vocal hook—an echo of the word “beings” whispered, layered, then slightly distorted—acts as a sonic mantra. Its placement at key structural points of the track serves two purposes. First, it **anchors the listener’s focus**, reminding them of the central question: who are we in this collective? Second, the gradual distortion of the hook as the song progresses mirrors the way identity can become warped when filtered through external lenses.

The title, therefore, is not just a label but an **active participant** in the narrative. Each time the hook resurfaces, it feels like a checkpoint where the narrator briefly re‑examines their stance. The effect is akin to a loop of self‑reflection that becomes increasingly nuanced with each repeat.

### Production as narrative device

Madeon’s production choices act like storytelling tools:

- **Layered textures** – The track begins with a thin, almost fragile synth pad that slowly accrues layers. This accumulation mirrors how societal expectations pile onto an individual, thickening the soundscape of the self.

- **Dynamic contrast** – Moments of sudden silence or stripped‑back bass create brief spaces for contemplation, allowing the emotional weight of the previous build‑up to settle before the next surge.

- **Sidechain compression** – The characteristic “pumping” effect, where the synths breathe with the kick drum, evokes a sense of being *in sync* with an external rhythm—an audible representation of the push‑and‑pull between personal desire and group tempo.

- **Glitchy artifacts** – Subtle digital glitches appear near the climax, suggesting moments of glitching identity—instances where the self momentarily breaks through the façade.

These production techniques make the listener feel the **push and pull of conformity**, turning an electronic track into a psychological journey.

### Fan interpretation and resonance

Online conversations about *Beings* often revolve around its relevance to the modern experience of **social media** and **online personas**. Many fans describe the song as the anthem for those who feel they are “performing” themselves for an audience—a feeling that aligns closely with the track’s lyrical subtext about being a “being” among many.

Some listeners tie the song to personal milestones: the moment they decided to leave a toxic group, the realization that they had been living for validation, or the acceptance of a new community that allowed them to keep a piece of their uniqueness. This multiplicity of personal narratives demonstrates how *Beings* functions as a **mirror**, reflecting different facets of the same underlying tension.

Additionally, the track’s uplifting finale provides a sense of **hopeful catharsis** that fans cherish. Rather than a bleak ending, the music suggests that integration does not have to erase individuality, an interpretation that has turned the song into a source of comfort for those navigating identity crises.

## FAQ

**Q: What does the repeated word “beings” actually represent?**  
A: It acts as a metaphor for the various masks and roles we adopt. Each repetition invites listeners to consider how many “beings” they are presenting to the world versus how many remain hidden.

**Q: Is the song about digital identity, or is it more universal?**  
A: While the production and lyrical mood lend themselves to a digital‑age reading, the core conflict—maintaining selfhood within a collective—is a timeless human dilemma that transcends any single era.

**Q: Why does the track feel both uplifting and melancholy at the same time?**  
A: The juxtaposition mirrors the dual nature of conformity: there is comfort in belonging (uplift) but also loss of personal nuance (melancholy). The melodic rises symbolize hope, while the darker bass reflects the underlying sadness.

**Q: Does the song suggest a solution to the tension it describes?**  
A: Rather than prescribing a concrete answer, it hints at acceptance. The final melodic release suggests that integration and individuality can coexist, albeit in an altered form.

**Q: How does the production reinforce the lyrical themes?**  
A: Layered synths, sidechain “pumping,” and glitchy textures each embody aspects of pressure, synchronization, and the occasional breakdown of identity, turning the sonic landscape into a narrative device.

**Q: Are there specific moments in the song that signify a shift in the narrator’s mindset?**  
A: The transition from the minimal opening pad to the full‑blown chorus marks a moment of self‑assertion, while the brief post‑chorus silence acts as a reflective pause before the final acceptance.

**Q: Why do fans connect this song with personal growth?**  
A: Because *Beings* frames the struggle of self‑definition as an ongoing process. Listeners often see their own journeys mirrored in the track’s rise, fall, and ultimate resolve, making it a soundtrack for moments of personal evolution.
