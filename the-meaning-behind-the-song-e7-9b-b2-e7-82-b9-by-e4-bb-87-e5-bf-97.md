- **Shadows and Light** – Throughout the track, shadows are depicted as “stretching further than the night,” while light is described as “a thin thread slipping through cracks.” Light operates as a metaphor for truth and consciousness; shadows represent the lingering doubts and unspoken fears that linger even when the lights are on. - **Broken Glass** – At one point, the narrator mentions stepping over broken glass, a clear nod to the idea that confronting a blind spot is painful and potentially hazardous. The glass also reflects the surrounding world in distorted fragments, symbolizing a distorted self‑perception that only a shattered view can reveal.

- **The Blind Spot Itself** – Beyond the literal visual phenomenon, the blind spot stands for **psychological myopia**—the inability to see the consequences of one’s actions because they reside outside of the field of conscious attention.
