The track is more than a catchy refrain; it is a reflective confession that asks, *what does it really mean to keep moving forward when the past keeps echoing in the present?* This tension, between forward motion and lingering reminiscence, is why the song warrants a deeper, lyrical excavation.

## The Emotional Core of *How It Be*  

### A Voice Between Hope and Hesitation  

At its heart, *How It Be* is spoken from the perspective of someone caught in a liminal space—neither fully past a heartbreak nor entirely entrenched in it. Breezy Bank’s vocal timbre oscillates between soft sighs and a more urgent, breath‑laden delivery, suggesting a **duality of yearning**: the desire to reclaim a lost intimacy, and the fear of re‑opening wounds that have begun to scar over. This push‑pull is evident whenever the narrator references moments that feel “still too close” despite the passage of time, conveying a **raw vulnerability** that refuses to be smoothed over by the glossy veneer of pop production.

The emotional conflict is not confined to romantic loss. Subtle hints of familial expectation and societal pressure seep through the verses, manifesting as a lingering **sense of inadequacy** that haunts the narrator. When the singer whispers about “walking streets that used to feel like home,” the emotional resonance shifts from nostalgia to an aching displacement—an acknowledgment that the familiar can become alien when the self has evolved. This juxtaposition of *comfort* and *alienation* is the emotional engine that powers the whole track.

### Desire for Resolution  

Another layer lies in the narrator’s yearning for closure. The repeated chorus, which cycles around a question of “how it be,” is less a rhetorical inquiry and more an **unspoken plea for an answer**. The phrase functions as a mantra, a way of trying to impose order on the chaotic swirl of memories and feelings. In doing so, Breezy Bank captures the universal human experience of **searching for meaning** in the aftermath of life‑changing events. The emotional core thus rests on an intimate dialogue with oneself—a conversation marked by both resignation and the faint spark of hope that perhaps, someday, the pieces will align.

## Main Themes and Message  

### The Weight of Memory  

One of the most dominant themes in *How It Be* is the **burden of memory**. The narrator recounts scenes that linger like photographs, each image carrying an emotional weight that refuses to dissolve. This creates a sense of time being **non‑linear**, where past and present collide in a single mental landscape. Breezy Bank seems to argue that memories are not merely recollections; they are active participants that shape current identity, influencing decisions and emotional responses long after the original events have faded.

### Identity as Flux  

Closely tied to memory is the notion of identity as something perpetually **in flux**. The song avoids presenting a static self; instead, it embraces transformation as a continuous process. When the narrator mentions “changing the colors of my skin,” it is a metaphor for shedding former selves—whether those are personas we adopt for others or masks we wear to survive. The message is clear: **growth requires confronting the parts of us we left behind**, even when it feels uncomfortable or painful.

### Acceptance Through Ambiguity  

Unlike many pop anthems that resolve conflict with a cathartic climax, *How It Be* embraces ambiguity. The lack of a definitive resolution in the lyrics reflects a **philosophical acceptance** that some questions remain unanswered. By repeatedly circling back to the central question, Breezy Bank suggests that living with uncertainty is itself a form of maturity—recognizing that the journey itself, rather than the destination, offers the most profound insight.

## Symbolism and Metaphors  

### Water as Fluidity and Emotional Depth  

Throughout the track, water recurs as a metaphor for emotional depth and the **ever‑changing nature of feelings**. References to rain, tides, and submerged thoughts indicate how the narrator’s inner world is constantly shifting—sometimes calm, sometimes turbulent. The lyric that paraphrases “the city’s river runs through my veins” merges the external landscape with internal biology, hinting that the environment and personal experience are intertwined, each shaping the other.

### Mirrors and Self‑Reflection  

Mirrors serve as another potent image. The narrator often describes looking into glass surfaces that “show a face I barely recognize.” This symbolism operates on two levels: first, as a literal act of self‑examination, and second, as a comment on how external perceptions can distort our sense of self. The mirror becomes a **dual‑sided object**—both a tool for honesty and a curse that reinforces self‑doubt, perfectly echoing the song’s central tension between clarity and confusion.

### Streets as Pathways of Memory  

When the verses wander through “empty streets that once sang with laughter,” the streets function as a **metaphorical map** of the past. Each block represents a chapter of the narrator’s life, now quiet and dimmed. The transformation of bustling avenues into ghostly corridors underscores the theme of **loss of communal belonging**, inviting listeners to consider how environments evolve alongside personal change.

## The Role of the Title and Hook  

The phrase “How It Be” operates as a linguistic shortcut, condensing existential contemplation into a catchy hook. By stripping the question down to its bare essentials, Breezy Bank makes the title **universally accessible**, allowing listeners from varied backgrounds to project their own dilemmas onto it. The repetition of this phrase throughout the track serves as an **anchor**, a moment where the chaotic lyrical landscape pauses, granting space for reflection.

Moreover, the informal construction—dropping the auxiliary verb “does” and the preposition “to”—mirrors the song’s casual, conversational tone. This linguistic choice signals intimacy, as if the narrator is confiding in a close friend rather than delivering a polished statement. Consequently, the title becomes both a **question and a mantra**, reinforcing the central theme that some truths are felt rather than spoken outright.

## Production and Sound Supporting the Narrative  

The sonic palette of *How It Be* is deliberately sparse, employing soft synth pads, a muted piano line, and a low‑frequency bass that pulses like a heartbeat. This minimalism mirrors the emotional vulnerability of the lyrics; there is little to distract from the **raw confession** at its core. The occasional reverberated vocal echo creates an impression of a voice bouncing off an empty hallway—symbolising the lingering presence of past conversations in the narrator’s mind.

A subtle shift occurs midway through the track, where a brushed snare brush surfaces, adding a hint of **forward momentum** without breaking the introspective mood. This production choice subtly underscores the lyrical theme of moving forward while still being tethered to what came before. The overall mix sits the vocals front‑and‑center, allowing Breezy Bank’s nuanced delivery to dictate the emotional arc, while the understated instrumentation acts as a supportive, almost therapeutic backdrop.

## Fan Interpretations and Why the Song Resonates  

Listeners have gravitated toward *How It Be* for its **relatable ambiguity**. Many fans describe the track as a soundtrack for moments when life feels unresolved—such as after a breakup, a career change, or the end of a long‑standing friendship. The song’s lack of a tidy resolution invites personal interpretation, making each listening experience a **mirror** reflecting the individual’s current emotional state.

The community around Breezy Bank frequently shares stories of how the song helped them articulate feelings they could not otherwise name. Its lyrical openness encourages fans to **project their own narratives** onto the verses, turning the track into a collective diary of shared uncertainty. This communal resonance amplifies the song’s impact, turning a personal confession into a cultural touchstone that validates the messy, unfinished chapters of human experience.

## FAQ  

**Q: What is the central question the narrator is asking in *How It Be*?**  
A: The song repeatedly circles around the idea of understanding how past experiences continue to shape present emotions. Rather than seeking a concrete answer, the narrator is probing the *process* of living with unresolved feelings and how they subtly influence daily life.

**Q: Are the water images literal or metaphorical?**  
A: While the references can evoke real weather or bodies of water, they function primarily as metaphors for emotional fluidity and depth. The tide’s rise and fall mirror the narrator’s fluctuating inner state, suggesting that emotions are as natural and inevitable as the ocean’s movements.

**Q: Why does the production feel so minimalistic?**  
A: The sparse arrangement strips away any distraction, allowing the lyrical content and vocal vulnerability to occupy the listener’s full attention. This production choice mirrors the theme of confronting one’s inner truth without the noise of external embellishment.

**Q: Does the song address romantic loss specifically?**  
A: Romantic loss is a powerful thread, but the lyrics are broad enough to encompass any significant relational separation—be it familial, friendship, or even a shift in self‑identity. The universality of the language invites multiple layers of interpretation.

**Q: How does the title “How It Be” enhance the song’s meaning?**  
A: The colloquial phrasing creates an intimate, conversational tone, positioning the question as a personal mantra rather than a formal inquiry. Its repetitive hook grounds the track, providing listeners with a lyrical anchor that reflects the ongoing internal dialogue.

**Q: Why do fans connect so strongly with the ambiguous ending?**  
A: The lack of resolution mirrors real life, where many questions remain open-ended. This authenticity validates listeners’ own unfinished narratives, making the song a shared space for processing uncertainty without forcing a tidy conclusion.

**Q: Can the street imagery be interpreted beyond nostalgia?**  
A: Yes. Streets symbolize pathways and life’s journey. The shift from bustling avenues to empty corridors underscores the feeling of isolation and the transformation of familiar spaces as one’s internal landscape evolves. This dual symbolism deepens the song’s exploration of change and memory.
