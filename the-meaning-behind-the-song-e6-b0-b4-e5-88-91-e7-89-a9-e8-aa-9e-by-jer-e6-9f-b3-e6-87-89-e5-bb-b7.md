## The Emotional Core: What the Narrator Feels, Wants, and Fears

## Main Themes and Message: From Regret to Self‑Reclamation
Two primary themes dominate the lyrical landscape: **impermanence** and **self‑reclamation**. The water motif underscores the Buddhist‑inspired notion that all things are transient. Yet, the act of forging a sword from that same water suggests a refusal to simply accept dissolution. The narrator seems to declare: *“If the world will not hold me, I will shape my own hold.”* This duality invites listeners to consider **the paradox of embracing change while demanding permanence for the self**.

Regret appears as a secondary, yet essential, theme. The narrator often reflects on missed opportunities, on words left unsaid that have now become echoes in a fluid void. However, regret does not linger as a static wound; it becomes a catalyst for the song’s forward motion. By turning regret into a forging fire, the track implies that **pain can be repurposed into creative force**, a message that resonates deeply with anyone who has felt stuck in a cycle of self‑blame.
