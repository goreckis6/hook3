## Symbolism and Metaphors

### Wind as Uncertainty and Freedom

Throughout the song, the wind is described with adjectives that range from “soft” to “tempestuous.”  This duality encodes the **simultaneous freedom and instability** that uncertainty brings.  In psychological terms, the wind can be seen as the **liminal space** where the self tests its boundaries.  

### Stars as Aspirational Beacons

Stars appear early in the title, immediately establishing a **cosmic perspective**.  They are distant yet visible, implying that **aspirations may be out of reach but still directional**.  By positioning the star before the wind, the song tells us that **hope precedes the journey**; it is the guiding light that initiates movement.  

### Flowers as Ephemeral Beauty

The flower motif is placed squarely in the middle of the title, functioning as the **tangible manifestation of inner growth**.  Flowers, by nature, bloom for a limited time, reinforcing the idea that **moments of joy are fleeting and must be cherished**.  The choice of a flower that “opens only under certain conditions” adds a layer of **conditional vulnerability**, implying that authentic expression often requires specific emotional climates.  

### Sun as Resolution and Acceptance

The sun concludes the title, delivering a **sense of completeness and warmth**.  Unlike the star’s distant coldness, the sun is immediate, nurturing, and life‑affirming.  Its placement signifies that after navigating the uncertainties (wind) and cultivating inner beauty (flower), the narrator reaches a place of **self‑acceptance and outward radiance**.
