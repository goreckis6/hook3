## The Emotional Core of the Song

### Rainy Streets and Neon Lights

The *rainy streets* that recur in the song serve a dual purpose. On one level, rain denotes **cleansing**, a natural desire for purification after a sin. Yet the streets are described as never drying, indicating that the cleansing is *perpetual and never complete*. Neon lights, conversely, represent **artificial illumination**—the bright, dazzling distractions that mask deeper darkness. Together, they paint a scene where *the protagonist is constantly bathed in a false sense of clarity while being drenched in unresolved guilt*.

### Broken Clocks and Time

A broken clock is used as a symbol of **timelessness and halted progress**. The narrator mentions “the hands stopped at a moment we can’t forget”—a vivid description of how trauma can freeze an individual in a particular emotional timestamp. This metaphor stresses that **the mistake has stolen the flow of future possibilities**, holding the character hostage to a past moment that refuses to be revised.
