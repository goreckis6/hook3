## The Emotional Core of the Song

The amber light becomes a metaphor for **hesitation**, a cautionary pause that forces the narrator to weigh the risks of moving forward versus staying put. The subsequent shift to green—though brief—signals a momentary surge of confidence, only to be followed by the inevitable red, reminding listeners that “green lights” are often fleeting, and that momentum can be rapidly halted by external forces.
