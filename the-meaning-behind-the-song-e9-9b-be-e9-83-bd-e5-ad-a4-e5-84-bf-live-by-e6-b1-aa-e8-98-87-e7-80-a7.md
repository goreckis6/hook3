### Main Themes and Message  
At first listen, the song may sound like a simple ode to nostalgia, yet deeper layers reveal a **complex dialogue on identity and self‑acceptance**. The recurring motif of “living by” something—whether it’s a memory, a promise, or an expectation—serves as a metaphor for how we allow external forces to dictate internal narratives.  

1. **Impermanence** – The track repeatedly hints that all moments, however cherished, are fleeting. This aligns with a Buddhist undertone frequently found in contemporary East‑Asian songwriting, where acceptance of impermanence can be both liberating and mournful.  
2. **Self‑definition through loss** – By confronting the loss of a specific feeling, the narrator discovers a **new sense of self** that is not solely defined by the past. There is an implied transformation—a shedding of old skin—that surfaces as the chorus swells.  
3. **The paradox of memory** – Memories are portrayed as both a sanctuary and a trap. The song asks whether we truly live by our memories or if we become imprisoned by them, urging listeners to consider how much of their present identity is tethered to what has already passed.

- **Horizon**: The distant line where sky meets earth evokes a **future that remains out of reach**. It represents hope, yet also the daunting expanse of the unknown that the narrator must confront.  
- **Silhouettes**: Shadows and outlines of figures appear throughout the song, standing for **parts of ourselves that are only partially visible**, hinting at the elusive nature of true self‑knowledge.  

These images are deliberately ambiguous, allowing each listener to insert their own narrative—be it a lost love, a gone‑by childhood, or an unfulfilled dream—into the symbolic frame.
