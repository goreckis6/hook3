Storm imagery—gusts, rain, an unsettled sky—appears throughout the track, symbolizing emotional turbulence. The narrator likens his mental state to a **storm that cannot be tamed**, portraying a sense of being buffeted by both internal and external forces. The storm serves as a visual reminder that the calm and composure displayed publicly may simply be a momentary lull before the next gust of self‑questioning hits.
