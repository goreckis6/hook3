### Main themes and message  

**Regret and redemption** are the most obvious pillars, yet the song also touches on **identity formation** and the **burden of expectations**. The lyricist uses that duality to ask whether the protagonist is *running from* who they were or *running toward* an idealized version that never fully materialized.  

Another layer emerges in the song’s **examination of memory**. In this way, the song comments on how **nostalgia can both soothe and trap**; the narrator appears to be simultaneously longing for the warmth of those memories and fearing that dwelling on them will prevent forward motion.

### The role of the title and hook in the meaning  

The title, a string of Korean characters that translates loosely to “In the shadows of the old school yard, chasing the Puma,” operates on two frequencies.  First, it **grounds the song in a specific cultural and spatial context**, hinting at a place where childhood games were once played—a schoolyard that now exists only in memory.  Second, the hook “Puma” is deliberately in English, a linguistic choice that mirrors the global, hybridized identity of many young Koreans navigating both local tradition and western pop culture.  By placing an English animal name at the forefront, the artist highlights **the tension between domestic roots and external influences**, reinforcing the larger narrative of trying to preserve authenticity while sprinting toward an ever‑changing horizon.  

The repeated chant of “Puma” during the chorus also acts as an **emotional anchor**.  Its percussive placement—right before the swelling synths—creates a momentary pause that feels like a breath held before a confession.  This micro‑structure mirrors the broader lyrical theme: the narrator takes a breath, gathers courage, and then rushes into an uncertain future, echoing the very act of chasing a fast, elusive creature.

**Q: Do fans interpret the song differently based on personal experience?**  
A: Yes.  Some view the Puma as a former lover; others see it as an abandoned career goal or a youthful version of themselves.  This flexibility is intentional, allowing the track to serve as a mirror for various “what‑ifs.”  

**Q: Can the song be understood as a critique of modern digital culture?**
