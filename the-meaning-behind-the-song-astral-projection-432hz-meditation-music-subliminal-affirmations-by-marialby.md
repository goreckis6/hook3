## Main Themes and Message  

### 1. **Alignment with Natural Order**  
The deliberate use of 432 hz is not a random production choice. Historically, this tuning is said to resonate with the planet’s natural frequencies, a notion that encourages a **re‑connection with an archetypal harmony**. In the context of the piece, it implies that true peace arises when we tune ourselves to the “natural” rhythm of our being.  

### 2. **Self‑Discovery through Subconscious Dialogue**  
The subliminal affirmations serve as a **quiet narrative** that runs parallel to the ambient soundscape. By embedding statements of value and possibility beneath the music, Marialby suggests that the path to self‑knowledge is often hidden beneath the surface of everyday awareness. The track’s message is that *listening deeply* can reveal inner truths that conscious mind may have overlooked.  

### 3. **Transcendence of Physical Limits**  
“**Astral Projection**” is a term borrowed from esoteric practice, referring to an out‑of‑body experience that allows the consciousness to travel beyond the physical vessel. Within the track, this concept becomes a **metaphor for mental expansion**: the listener is encouraged to leave behind limiting beliefs as if shedding a skin, moving into an expansive mental plane where possibilities feel limitless.  

### 4. **Healing as an Ongoing Process**  
Rather than presenting a final resolution, the music remains open‑ended, looping gently without a definitive climax. This structure mirrors the idea that **healing is cyclical**, a practice repeated daily. The refrain of gentle affirmation reinforces the notion that growth is sustained through continual, subtle reinforcement.

## Production and Sound as Narrative  

Marialby’s production choices are not decorative; they are integral to the piece’s **storytelling**. The slow attack and long decay of the synth pads mimic the ebb and flow of breath, an essential anchor for meditation. The subtle **reverberation** creates a sense of spaciousness, evoking the feeling of being suspended in a vast cosmos—a sonic representation of astral travel.  

The **frequency spectrum** is deliberately balanced: the low end remains warm and constant, while higher frequencies shimmer faintly, suggesting fleeting thoughts that brush the periphery of consciousness. This balance encourages the listener to *stay grounded yet remain open* to transcendental impressions.  

Importantly, the **subliminal affirmations** are mixed just beneath the threshold of conscious hearing, positioned around -25 dB relative to the main ambient layer. This placement ensures they influence the listener’s *pre‑attentive processing* without interrupting the meditative flow, mirroring how deep‑seated beliefs operate beneath our awareness.
