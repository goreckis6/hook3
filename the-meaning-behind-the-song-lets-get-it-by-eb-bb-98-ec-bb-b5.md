## Main Themes and Message

### 1. The Paradox of Freedom

The lyricism hints at a yearning for liberation that is simultaneously called into question. The protagonist wants to “get it by”—to surpass obstacles and seize opportunity—yet the words are undercut by hints of **attachment to the familiar**. This paradox mirrors real‑world experiences where individuals chase independence while still craving the safety net of known structures. The song suggests that true freedom is not an absolute state but a **continuous negotiation** between moving forward and holding onto parts of the past.

### 2. Identity Through Performance

Another nuanced theme is the **construction of identity through performance**. The repetitive chant in the chorus feels like a stage direction, inviting listeners to join the performance. By encouraging collective participation, the song posits that identity is reinforced when we are seen and heard in a communal setting. The narrator's confidence crescendos as the audience echoes the hook, indicating that self‑belief can be **cultivated through shared experience** rather than solitary resolve.

### 3. The Burden and Blessing of Ambition

Ambition is portrayed as a double‑edged sword: it drives progress but also imposes a weight that the narrator feels keenly. The undercurrent of **fatigue** in the verses suggests that the pursuit of success has already exacted a toll. Yet the throw‑away line that urges “let’s get it by” is a **defiant reclamation**—the narrator chooses to reframe exhaustion as a badge of honor, a sign that they are *still in the fight*.
